The V364 word list - Courtesy Bo Zimmerman and Richard Atkinson.
================== - Updated by Steve Gray 2026-09-16

There are 261 words, though quite a few are homonyms (three fewer than the magic voice: bye/buy,
here/hear, and oh/o).  The word "grey" was replaced by "pink".

You can do:

	SAY number           - From 0 to 260, ie: SAY 84  will say "COMMODORE"
	SAY "word"           - Unknown words will cause an error.
	SAY "word word ..."  - Separate every "word" with a space. Do not use punctuation!

The following "partial" words can not be used inside quotes. These include:

	thir, fif, teen, ty, est, ing, ssss, th   (ssss being used to make plural)

This differs from the Magic Voice behavior. These should be used with their word NUMBER, ie: SAY 69

Additional forms of some words will be listed with a hyphen but should be written with a SPACE in between
or using their word NUMBER. Sound-alike words will be enclosed in quotes.
